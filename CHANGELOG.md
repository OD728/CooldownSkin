# Cooldown Skin changelog

## 1.1

- **Color menu** in `/cskin`: every color is a swatch, one row per theme, instead of a button to click through. Hover a swatch for its name; your current pick has a white ring, and the swatches follow the Brightness setting.
- Each row has a striped swatch for the whole set (every spell gets its own color pair) and six single pairs (one pair for every spell). Single pairs are new.
- New color sets: **Gruvbox, Rosé Pine, Kanagawa, OneDark and Tokyo Night**, plus **Classic** (the game's own bar colors: health, mana, rage, energy, cast bar, rested) and **Item quality** (uncommon to heirloom).
- The color rows are in the same order as in the author's other addons.

## 1.0

First release.

- Square, cropped Cooldown Manager icons (Essential, Utility, Tracked Buffs) in a 2 px black border.
- While your debuff or buff is up: no yellow wash, and a dark swipe grows as it runs out. The border stays black; `/cskin highlight` colors it instead.
- A thin timer bar above the icon while the debuff is up, in neon gradients inside a thin black edge, with a spark at its end (`/cskin barcolor`, `/cskin barglow`). Toned down to 70% brightness by default (`/cskin barbright`). Color sets where each spell gets its own pair: Neon mix, Catppuccin (Mocha pastels), Cyberpunk and Dracula. Covers your debuffs on an enemy, your HoTs and buffs on a friendly target, and buffs on yourself, whichever rank you cast.
- Outlined timer numbers sized to the icon.
- Tracked Bars skinned to match: square icon and bar in black edges, the bar in the same neon gradients with a spark, crisp text.
- An options window: `/cskin`, the minimap addon menu, or Options > AddOns > Cooldown Skin. The commands still work (`/cskin help`).
