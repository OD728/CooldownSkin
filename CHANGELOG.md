# Cooldown Skin changelog

## 1.0

First release.

- Square, cropped Cooldown Manager icons (Essential, Utility, Tracked Buffs) in a 2 px black border.
- While your debuff or buff is up: no yellow wash, the border takes your class color, and a dark swipe grows as it runs out.
- A thin class-colored timer bar above the icon while the debuff is up.
- Outlined timer numbers sized to the icon.
- Options with `/cskin`.
