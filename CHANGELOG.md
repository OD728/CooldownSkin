# Cooldown Skin changelog

## 1.0

First release.

- Square, cropped Cooldown Manager icons (Essential, Utility, Tracked Buffs) in a 2 px black border.
- While your debuff or buff is up: no yellow wash, and a dark swipe grows as it runs out. The border stays black; `/cskin highlight` colors it instead.
- A thin timer bar above the icon while the debuff is up, in neon gradients inside a thin black edge, with a spark at its end (`/cskin barcolor`, `/cskin barglow`). Covers your debuffs on an enemy, your HoTs and buffs on a friendly target, and buffs on yourself, whichever rank you cast.
- Outlined timer numbers sized to the icon.
- Options with `/cskin`.
